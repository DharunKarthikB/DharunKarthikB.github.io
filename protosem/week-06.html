PHASE 02 • WEEK 06

Geometric Horse Design

Laser Cutting and Engraving

Project Overview

Modified an existing geometric horse design using RDWorks V8 and executed it on black acrylic measuring 50 × 50 mm. The project involved both laser cutting and engraving to produce an intricate geometric artwork featuring triangular patterns.

  • Software Used: RDWorks V8
  • Material: Black Acrylic – 50 × 50 mm

Design and Execution

  • Imported an existing geometric horse design into RDWorks V8.
  • Modified the original design to suit the intended output.
  • Prepared the design for laser cutting and engraving.
  • Used the software preview to inspect the layout before processing.
  • Executed the design on black acrylic to produce the final artwork.

Project Outcome

Successfully produced a geometric horse artwork incorporating detailed triangular patterns through laser cutting and engraving. The project demonstrated practical experience in modifying digital designs, preparing laser processing layouts, and translating digital artwork into a physical output.
